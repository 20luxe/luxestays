Our Policies

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in is from 2pm and check-out is by 11am. If you need to arrive earlier, message us on WhatsApp and we will try to help.

Location & Areas

Luxe Stays is located in Kisaasi and Kyanja. We serve students, tourists and families looking for short or long stays in these areas.

Cancellation (short notice)

Cancel 48 hours before your stay for a full refund. Cancel later for a half refund. Tell us within 24 hours if something is not right with your room, and we will fix it or refund you.

Privacy Notice (short)

We only use your phone number, name and stay dates to process and confirm your booking. We do not sell your information to anyone.

Terms (short)

By booking with us, you agree to pay the agreed price and to give a correct phone number and stay dates. Prices may change, and we will always tell you the price before you pay.
