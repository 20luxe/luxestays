Our Story

Luxe Stays started with a simple idea: everyone deserves a clean, comfortable place to stay at a fair price. We noticed that students, tourists and families in Kisaasi and Kyanja often struggled to find affordable accommodation that felt like home. So we opened Luxe Stays to offer both short and long stays with friendly service and reliable comfort.

Our Values

  • Fresh: every room is cleaned and ready before you arrive.
  • Friendly: we treat every guest with respect and kindness.
  • Reliable: we deliver the stay we promise, on time.

Who we serve

Students looking for affordable long stays, tourists visiting Kampala, and families who need a comfortable short stay — all are welcome at Luxe Stays.
