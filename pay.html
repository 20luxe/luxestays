How to Pay & Book

Three simple steps

  1. Send us a WhatsApp message with the room you want and your dates.
  2. Choose a payment method below and pay.
  3. Send us the payment confirmation so we can confirm your booking.

Note: This is a student project, payments are not active.

How we confirm a booking

After you pay, send us a screenshot or the confirmation message on WhatsApp. We check it, reply to confirm your booking, and then hold your room for your dates.

Our refund rule

If you cancel at least 48 hours before your stay, we refund your money in full. If you cancel later, we refund half. If a room is not as described, tell us within 24 hours and we will fix it or refund you.
